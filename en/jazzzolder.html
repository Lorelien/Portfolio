Jazzzolder Mechelen — Website Redesign

For this group project, I redesigned the website of Jazzzolder, a historic jazz club in the Predikherenkerk in Mechelen. We improved the website structure, ticket sales and visual identity.

My role included UX/UI design, designing brand sheets and pages in Figma, and the final implementation in WordPress with Elementor.

The assignment

The existing website contained a lot of text, used many frames and had an unclear structure. Not all navigation links worked and important content, such as photos and the guestbook, was difficult to find.

Our assignment was to preserve the rich history, route description and concert information, while making the website clearer, more attractive and easier to use.

About Jazzzolder

Jazzzolder is a jazz club in the Predikherenkerk in Mechelen. Every second and fourth Friday of the month, the club organises concerts with Belgian and international jazz musicians.

The website supports both regular visitors and new interested people. Visitors should be able to quickly find concerts, practical information and the history of Jazzzolder. There is also merchandise that visitors can order during concert evenings.

Research and briefing

The project started with a briefing from Jazzzolder and a PowerPoint with content requirements. The organisation indicated which information had to remain and which sections on the existing website were important.

We also attended a concert evening. By experiencing the location, visitors and atmosphere ourselves, we were able to design a website that is not only functional, but also fits the warm and cultural character of Jazzzolder.

Brand sheet and visual identity

We kept the existing logo and developed a brand sheet with a warm, energetic colour palette and clear typography. Dark text on a light background ensures readability, while yellow, red and dark accents emphasise important actions and titles.

For typography we chose Outfit for headings and navigation, and Sora for body text and call-to-actions. This combination creates a clear hierarchy and a recognisable, contemporary look.

Figma prototype

In Figma we designed a website with pages for concert posters, gallery, merchandise, tickets and information about Jazzzolder. We introduced a clearer structure, added more atmospheric images and used call-to-actions to guide visitors to relevant content.

A new component was the merchandise page. Visitors can choose a pattern, product type, colour and details via a clear step-by-step flow. This makes the made-to-order process understandable and low-threshold.

Adjustments after feedback

After feedback from Jazzzolder and the lecturers, we improved the navigation, readability and visual consistency. We removed the login page, made the logo visible while scrolling and added a direct agenda link to the navigation.

We increased the text size, improved line height, added consistent rounded corners and reduced unnecessary frames. On desktop we replaced swipe interactions with clear carousels with arrows.

We also made sponsor logos clickable, added bus information to the route planner and improved the contact page with an attachment button and a teaser for the next concert.

Implementation in WordPress

In the final phase we built the website in WordPress with Elementor. We divided the pages, navigation and footer within the team and adapted the design where technical limitations required it.

For example, the merchandise flow was split into separate steps with a “Next” button. The event page and poster carousel also received a custom implementation due to Elementor’s capabilities. Despite these changes, the website remained intuitive and recognisable.

My contribution

Within the team, I contributed to analysing the existing website, processing the briefing, developing the brand sheet, creating high-fidelity designs in Figma and the implementation in WordPress. I helped structure the content and translate Jazzzolder’s identity into a clear digital experience.

I designed the About Us page and the merchandise page in Figma. In addition, I contributed to the WordPress implementation of the merchandise page and the About Us page.
