Cookie Policy

On this website, the portfolio of Lorelien Michiels, a limited amount of technology is used to understand how visitors use the website. I respect your privacy and ask for consent before enabling analytical tracking.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website can store on your device. They can be necessary to make a website work correctly or used to gain insight into the use of a website.

Which cookies does this website use?

This website does not use advertising or marketing cookies. When you give consent, the website may use analytical technology from Contentsquare. Contentsquare helps me to analyse aggregated click, scroll and navigation behaviour, so that I can improve the user experience of my portfolio.

Analytical cookies

Analytical cookies are only enabled after you give consent via the cookie banner. If you refuse, the Contentsquare tracking code will not load and you can still fully use the website.

The collected information is used to analyse which pages, buttons and parts of my portfolio visitors use. I use these insights solely to improve my portfolio and user experience.

Changing your choice

You can withdraw your consent at any time. To do this, delete the stored website data or local storage of this website in your browser. On your next visit, the cookie banner will appear again and you can make a new choice.
