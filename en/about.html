Hello, I’m Lorelien!

I’m a third-year Digital Experience Design student at Thomas More in Mechelen, specialising in UX/UI design and User-Centered Design. I love turning ideas and user research into logical wireframes, polished design systems and interactive Figma prototypes.

In my projects, I focus on the combination of visual hierarchy, intuitive interaction and digital accessibility (WCAG). I make sure interfaces are pleasant and inclusive for every end user.

How I work

As a designer, I’m a calm, focused worker who enjoys working independently and has an eye for detail. At the same time, I collaborate easily and pleasantly in a team: I take initiative where needed, discuss openly with team members and ask for advice or feedback in time to reach the best result together.

While I like to add a personal touch to my own portfolio with self-made line drawings, I naturally adapt completely to the brand identity, needs and wishes of the client on client projects.

Looking for an internship

For the second semester of my final year (February to May 2027), I am looking for a rewarding internship in the Mechelen or Leuven area, or somewhere easily accessible by public transport from Mechelen.

Outside of design

When I’m not behind my laptop screen designing in Figma, you’ll probably find me on the couch with a good book or at the cinema watching a film.

I love stories — whether through books, films, series or writing. I’m also a big cat lover and an introvert.
